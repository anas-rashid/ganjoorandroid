The app is now called Khazeena (خزینه, a treasury). Nothing about the reading changed — the old name was ganjoor.net's own, and carrying it made this look like their official app, which it never was.

Please uninstall the old Ganjoor app and use this one from now on; the old one gets no further updates. Android treats them as separate apps, so bookmarks and downloaded poets do not carry across. Copy any bookmarks you want to keep before removing it.
