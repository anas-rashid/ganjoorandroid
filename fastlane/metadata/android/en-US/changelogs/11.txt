Downloads keep going when you leave the app, with a notification showing their progress, and the Downloads page shows each poet being downloaded and those waiting. A failed download no longer closes the app; poems that did not arrive are counted, and a retry fetches just those. Poets that loaded slowly or not at all, such as Saeb and Iqbal, now open. A recitation lights up the couplet being read and follows it down the page. Changing the language in settings applies at once.
